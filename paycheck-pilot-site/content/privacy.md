## Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters, and this policy is written in plain English on purpose. Here's what happens with your information on The Paycheck Pilot.

### Information we collect

**You actively give us:**
- **Contact form messages** — your name, email address, and message content when you contact us. Used only to reply to you.

**Collected automatically:**
- **Basic analytics** — we use privacy-friendly, cookie-light analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to see aggregate patterns like which articles are popular. This does not use cross-site tracking cookies and does not identify you personally.
- **Server logs** — like every website, our host logs standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and troubleshooting. Logs are retained briefly and not used to profile visitors.

**We do not** run accounts, comments, or newsletters at this time, so we don't store passwords or preference profiles.

### Affiliate links and third parties

Some links on this site are affiliate links to third-party merchants (banks, budgeting apps, retailers). When you click one, you leave our site and the merchant's own privacy policy applies. We don't control their data practices — review their policies before signing up. We receive only aggregate referral counts from affiliate networks, never your personal or financial details.

### Cookies

This site itself sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content is minimal by design. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

### How we use information

- To respond to messages you send us.
- To understand which content is useful (aggregate analytics only).
- To keep the site secure and functional.

We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal information with anyone, except as required by law.

### Data retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. Analytics data is aggregated and retained per our analytics provider's standard retention.

### Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at [hello@thepaycheckpilot.com](mailto:hello@thepaycheckpilot.com). Depending on where you live (including California under CCPA/CPRA and the EU/UK under GDPR), you may have additional rights — we honor them all.

### Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date below and note the change here. Minor clarifications may be made without notice.
